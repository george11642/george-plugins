# Copywriting & Copy Editing

## Core Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness** -- choose clear when you must choose
2. **Benefits over features** -- connect every feature to an outcome
3. **Specificity over vagueness** -- "Cut reporting from 4h to 15min" beats "save time"
4. **Customer language** -- mirror voice-of-customer from reviews, interviews, support tickets
5. **One idea per section** -- each section advances one argument
6. **Active voice** -- "We generate reports" not "Reports are generated"
7. **Honest over sensational** -- never fabricate statistics or testimonials

## Headline Formulas

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| {Outcome} without {pain} | Understand user behavior without drowning in numbers |
| The {opposite} way to {outcome} | The easiest way to turn passion into income |
| Never {unpleasant event} again | Never miss a sales opportunity again |
| {Feature} for {audience} | Advanced analytics for Shopify e-commerce |
| {Feature} for {audience} to {use} | Online whiteboard for teams to ideate together |
| You don't have to {skill} to {outcome} | You don't need to be an SEO pro to rank higher |
| {Outcome} by {how} | Generate more leads by seeing who visits your site |
| {Key benefit} | Sound clear in online meetings |
| {Question highlighting pain} | Hate returning stuff to Amazon? |
| Turn {input} into {output} | Turn hard-earned sales into repeat customers |
| {Outcome} in {timeframe} | Launch your store in 10 minutes |
| The {category} that {differentiator} | The CRM that actually gets used |
| Stop {pain}. Start {pleasure}. | Stop guessing. Start knowing. |
| {Number} {people} use {product} to {outcome} | 10,000+ teams use Acme to ship faster |

## Page Structure (Landing Page)

### Strong page flow:
1. Hero with clear value prop
2. Social proof bar (logos or stats)
3. Problem/pain section
4. How it works (3 steps)
5. Key benefits (2-3, not 10)
6. Testimonial
7. Use cases or personas
8. Comparison to alternatives
9. Case study snippet
10. FAQ
11. Final CTA with guarantee

### Above the Fold
- **Headline**: single most important message, specific > generic
- **Subheadline**: expands headline, 1-2 sentences
- **Primary CTA**: communicate what they get, not what they do
- **Supporting visual**: reinforce message, not distract

### Section Types to Mix In
- How It Works (numbered steps)
- Competitor comparison
- Founder manifesto / story
- Testimonials (quotes, video, tweets)
- Use cases / personas
- Stats and social proof
- Demo / product tour
- FAQ
- Integrations / partners
- Guarantee / risk reversal

## CTA Patterns

**Weak**: Submit, Sign Up, Learn More, Click Here, Get Started

**Strong**: Start Free Trial, Get [Specific Thing], See [Product] in Action, Create Your First [Thing], Book My Demo

**Formula**: [Action Verb] + [What They Get] + [Qualifier]
- "Start My Free Trial"
- "Get the Complete Checklist"
- "See Pricing for My Team"

## Style Rules

### Words to cut
Very, really, extremely, incredibly, just, actually, basically, in order to, things, stuff

### Words to replace

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Utilize | Use |
| Implement | Set up |
| Leverage | Use |
| Facilitate | Help |
| Innovative | New |
| Robust | Strong |
| Seamless | Smooth |
| Cutting-edge | Modern |

### Sentence rules
- One idea per sentence
- Vary length (mix short and long)
- Front-load important info
- Max 3 conjunctions
- Usually under 25 words

## Seven Sweeps (Copy Editing Framework)

Sequential passes, each one dimension. After each sweep, re-check previous sweeps.

### Sweep 1: Clarity
Can the reader understand it? Check: confusing structures, unclear pronouns, jargon, ambiguity, missing context. Confirm Rule of One (one idea per section) and You Rule (speaks to reader).

### Sweep 2: Voice & Tone
Is it consistent? Check: shifts between formal/casual, inconsistent personality, jarring mood changes, random technical language.

### Sweep 3: So What
Does every claim answer "why should I care?" For every statement, ask "So what?" -- if copy doesn't answer with a deeper benefit, add the bridge.
- Bad: "Our platform uses AI-powered analytics"
- Good: "AI-powered analytics surface insights you'd miss manually -- so you make better decisions in half the time"

### Sweep 4: Prove It
Is every claim supported? Types of proof: testimonials, case studies, stats, third-party validation, guarantees, logos, review scores. Flag: "trusted by thousands" (which?), "industry-leading" (says who?).

### Sweep 5: Specificity
Is it concrete enough?

| Vague | Specific |
|---|---|
| Save time | Save 4 hours every week |
| Many customers | 2,847 teams |
| Fast results | Results in 14 days |
| Great support | Response within 2 hours |

### Sweep 6: Heightened Emotion
Does it make them feel something? Techniques: paint "before" state vividly, sensory language, micro-stories, shared experiences, reflective questions. Emotion serves the message, not manipulation.

### Sweep 7: Zero Risk
Have we removed every barrier? Check near CTAs for: unanswered objections, missing trust signals, unclear next steps, hidden costs. Add: guarantees, free trial terms, "no credit card required," social proof near CTA.

## Common Copy Problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Wall of features | Add "which means..." after each feature |
| Corporate speak | Ask "How would a human say this?" |
| Weak opening | Lead with reader's problem or desired outcome |
| Buried CTA | Make CTA obvious, early, and repeated |
| No proof | Add testimonials, numbers, case references |
| Generic claims | Specify who, how, and by how much |
| Mixed audiences | Pick one audience, write directly to them |
| Feature overload | Focus on 3-5 key benefits |

## Page-Specific Notes

- **Homepage**: serve multiple audiences without being generic; balance "ready to buy" and "still researching"
- **Landing page**: single message, single CTA, match headline to traffic source, remove nav
- **Pricing page**: help visitors choose; clarify inclusions; make recommended plan obvious
- **Feature page**: connect feature --> benefit --> outcome; differentiate from competitors
- **About page**: tell the why story; connect mission to customer benefit; still include CTA
