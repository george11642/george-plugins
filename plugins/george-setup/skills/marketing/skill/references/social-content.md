# Social Media Content

## Platform Strategy

### LinkedIn
- **Best for**: B2B, thought leadership, professional networking
- **Frequency**: 3-5x/week
- **What works**: personal stories with business lessons, contrarian takes, behind-the-scenes, data/insights, carousels, polls
- **What fails**: overly promotional, generic quotes, links in post body, corporate speak
- **Tips**: first line is everything (hook before "see more"), 1,200-1,500 chars, links in comments, tag sparingly
- **Algorithm**: first-hour engagement matters most; comments > reactions > clicks; dwell time signals quality; document posts get strong reach

### Twitter/X
- **Best for**: tech, media, real-time commentary, community
- **Frequency**: 3-10x/day (including replies)
- **What works**: hot takes, teaching threads, behind-the-scenes, engaging others, memes (if on-brand)
- **What fails**: pure self-promotion, threads without strong hook, ignoring replies, all-scheduled
- **Tips**: tweets under 100 chars get more engagement; quote tweets with insight > plain retweets
- **Algorithm**: replies and QTs build authority; threads rewarded; engagement in first 30 min matters

### Instagram
- **Best for**: visual brands, lifestyle, e-commerce, 18-44 demographic
- **Frequency**: 1-2 feed posts/day, 3-10 Stories/day
- **What works**: high-quality visuals, Stories, Reels, value carousels, UGC, interactive Stories
- **Tips**: Reels get 2x reach of static; first frame must hook; carousels: 10 educational slides
- **Algorithm**: Reels heavily prioritized; saves and shares > likes; consistency > perfection

### TikTok
- **Best for**: brand awareness, younger audiences (16-34), viral potential
- **Frequency**: 1-4x/day
- **What works**: native unpolished content, trending sounds, educational + entertaining, POV/day-in-life
- **What fails**: overly produced, ignoring trends, hard selling, repurposed horizontal video
- **Tips**: hook in first 1-2 seconds, under 30 seconds to start, vertical only (9:16)

### Facebook
- **Best for**: communities, local businesses, 25-55+ demographic
- **Frequency**: 1-2x/day
- **What works**: Groups, native video, live video, local content, discussion questions
- **What fails**: external links, promotional content, ignoring comments, cross-posting without adapting

## Content Pillars

Build content around 3-5 pillars. Example for a SaaS founder:

| Pillar | % | Topics |
|---|---|---|
| Industry insights | 30% | Trends, data, predictions |
| Behind-the-scenes | 25% | Building the company, lessons |
| Educational | 25% | How-tos, frameworks, tips |
| Personal | 15% | Stories, values, hot takes |
| Promotional | 5% | Product updates, offers |

## Hook Formulas

### Curiosity
- "I was wrong about [common belief]."
- "The real reason [outcome] happens isn't what you think."
- "[Impressive result] -- and it only took [surprisingly short time]."
- "Nobody talks about [insider knowledge]."

### Story
- "Last week, [unexpected thing] happened."
- "I almost [big mistake/failure]."
- "3 years ago, I [past state]. Today, [current state]."
- "[Person] told me something I'll never forget."

### Value
- "How to [outcome] (without [pain]):"
- "[Number] [things] that [outcome]:"
- "The simplest way to [outcome]:"
- "Stop [common mistake]. Do this instead:"

### Contrarian
- "Unpopular opinion: [bold statement]"
- "[Common advice] is wrong. Here's why:"
- "I stopped [common practice] and [positive result]."

### Social Proof
- "We [achieved result] in [timeframe]. Here's how:"
- "[Number] people asked me about [topic]. Here's my answer:"

## Post Templates

### LinkedIn: Story Post
```
[Hook: Unexpected outcome or lesson]
[Set the scene]
[The challenge]
[What you tried / what happened]
[The turning point]
[The result]
[The lesson for readers]
[Question to prompt engagement]
```

### LinkedIn: Contrarian Take
```
[Unpopular opinion stated boldly]
Here's why:
[Reason 1]
[Reason 2]
[Reason 3]
[What you recommend instead]
[Invite discussion: "Am I wrong?"]
```

### LinkedIn: List Post
```
[X things I learned about [topic] after [credibility builder]:
1. [Point] -- [Brief explanation]
2. [Point] -- [Brief explanation]
3. [Point] -- [Brief explanation]
[Wrap-up insight]
Which resonates most?
```

### Twitter/X: Tutorial Thread
```
Tweet 1: [Hook + promise of value]
Tweets 2-7: [One step per tweet with details]
Final: [Summary + "Follow me for more on [topic]"]
```

### Twitter/X: Breakdown Thread
```
Tweet 1: [Company/person] just [did thing]. Here's why it's genius:
Tweets 2-6: [Analysis points]
Tweet 7: [Your key takeaway + follow CTA]
```

## Content Repurposing System

### Blog Post --> Social
| Platform | Format |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Key insight + link in comments |
| LinkedIn | Carousel of main points |
| Twitter/X | Thread of key takeaways |
| Twitter/X | Single tweet with hot take |
| Instagram | Carousel with visuals |
| Instagram | Reel summarizing the post |

### Podcast/Video --> Social
| Platform | Format |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Quote graphic + insight |
| Twitter/X | Thread of best quotes |
| Instagram | Clip as Reel |
| TikTok | Short clip with caption |
| YouTube | Shorts from best moments |

### Workflow
1. Create pillar content (blog, video, podcast)
2. Extract 3-5 key insights
3. Adapt to each platform (format and tone)
4. Schedule across the week
5. Reshare evergreen content

## Voice Principles

- **Smart friend** -- write like texting advice; share discoveries, not lectures
- **Specific > vague** -- "$47,329" not "good revenue"; "47 days" not "a while"
- **Short. Breathe. Land.** -- one idea per sentence; line breaks liberally; let important points stand alone
- **Write from emotion** -- start with how you felt; use: frustrated, excited, terrified, obsessed

## Engagement Strategy

**Daily routine (30 min):**
1. Respond to all comments on your posts (5 min)
2. Comment on 5-10 target accounts (15 min)
3. Share/repost with added insight (5 min)
4. Send 2-3 DMs to new connections (5 min)

**Quality comments**: add new insight, share related experience, ask thoughtful follow-up, respectfully disagree with nuance. Never just "Great post!"

## Reverse Engineering Viral Content

1. **Niche ID**: Find 10-20 top creators with high engagement rate
2. **Scrape**: Collect 500+ posts using Apify, Phantom Buster, or manual
3. **Analyze**: Rank by engagement, identify top 10% patterns (hooks, formats, lengths, CTAs)
4. **Playbook**: Document repeatable hook, format, and CTA patterns
5. **Layer voice**: Apply your authentic voice using specificity, brevity, emotion
6. **Convert**: Bridge engagement to business -- newsletter signups, DM triggers, contextual product mentions
