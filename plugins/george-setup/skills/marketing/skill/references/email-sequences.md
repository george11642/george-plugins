# Email Sequences

## Core Principles

1. **One email, one job** -- one primary purpose, one main CTA
2. **Value before ask** -- lead with usefulness, earn the right to sell
3. **Relevance over volume** -- fewer, better emails win
4. **Clear path forward** -- every email moves them somewhere

---

## Sequence Templates

### Welcome Sequence (Post-Signup) -- 7 emails, 14 days

| # | Timing | Subject Pattern | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Immediate | Welcome to [Product] -- here's your first step | Deliver promise, set expectations |
| 2 | Day 1-2 | Get your first [result] in 10 minutes | Enable quick win |
| 3 | Day 3-4 | Why we built [Product] | Origin story, emotional connection |
| 4 | Day 5-6 | How [Customer] achieved [Result] | Social proof, case study |
| 5 | Day 7-8 | "I don't have time for X" -- sound familiar? | Overcome objection |
| 6 | Day 9-11 | Have you tried [Feature] yet? | Highlight underused capability |
| 7 | Day 12-14 | Ready to [upgrade/buy/commit]? | Conversion with risk reversal |

### Lead Nurture Sequence (Pre-Sale) -- 8 emails, 21 days

| # | Timing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Immediate | Deliver lead magnet + intro |
| 2 | Day 2-3 | Related insight, establish expertise |
| 3 | Day 4-5 | Problem deep-dive, show understanding |
| 4 | Day 6-8 | Solution framework (educational, not salesy) |
| 5 | Day 9-11 | Case study with real results |
| 6 | Day 12-14 | Differentiation -- why your approach is different |
| 7 | Day 15-18 | Objection handler / FAQ |
| 8 | Day 19-21 | Direct offer with strong value prop |

### Re-Engagement Sequence -- 4 emails

| # | Timing | Subject Pattern | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 30-60 inactive | Is everything okay, [Name]? | Genuine check-in |
| 2 | +2-3 days | Remember when you [achieved X]? | Remind of value + what's new |
| 3 | +5-7 days | We miss you -- here's something special | Incentive offer |
| 4 | +10-14 days | Should we stop emailing you? | Clean list if no response |

### Onboarding Sequence (Product Users) -- 7 emails

| # | Timing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Immediate | Confirm signup + one critical action |
| 2 | Day 1 | Getting started help (if step 1 incomplete) |
| 3 | Day 2-3 | Feature highlight with use case |
| 4 | Day 4-5 | Customer success story |
| 5 | Day 7 | Check-in, ask for feedback |
| 6 | Day 10-12 | Advanced tip / power feature |
| 7 | Day 14+ | Upgrade/expand prompt |

## Lifecycle Email Types (Audit Checklist)

### Onboarding
- [ ] New user welcome series
- [ ] New customer (paid) series
- [ ] Key onboarding step reminders (triggered by incomplete actions)
- [ ] Team invite sequence

### Retention
- [ ] Upgrade to paid (trial ending or behavior-based)
- [ ] Upgrade to higher plan (approaching limits)
- [ ] Ask for review (after positive milestone, NOT after bugs)
- [ ] Proactive support (usage dropped, failed actions)
- [ ] Product usage report (weekly/monthly value demonstration)
- [ ] NPS survey (quarterly; follow up: promoters --> review ask, detractors --> personal outreach)
- [ ] Referral program

### Billing
- [ ] Switch to annual (calculate exact savings, one-click switch)
- [ ] Failed payment recovery (4 emails over 14 days, assume accident)
- [ ] Cancellation survey (learn why, offer targeted save)
- [ ] Renewal reminder (14-30 days before, no surprise charges)

### Win-Back
- [ ] Expired trial (4 emails over 30 days, segment by engagement level)
- [ ] Cancelled customers (3 emails over 90 days, personalize by cancellation reason)

### Campaigns
- [ ] Monthly roundup / newsletter
- [ ] Seasonal promotions (announcement --> reminder --> last chance)
- [ ] Product updates (target by relevance)
- [ ] Pricing changes (30-60 days notice, grandfather if possible)

## Subject Line Patterns

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Question | Still struggling with X? |
| How-to | How to [outcome] in [timeframe] |
| Number | 3 ways to [benefit] |
| Direct | [First name], your [thing] is ready |
| Story tease | The mistake I made with [topic] |

**Rules**: clear > clever, specific > vague, 40-60 characters, test emoji (polarizing)

**Preview text**: extends subject line, ~90-140 chars, don't repeat subject, complete the thought or add intrigue

---

## Email Copy Structure

1. **Hook**: first line grabs attention
2. **Context**: why this matters to them
3. **Value**: the useful content
4. **CTA**: what to do next
5. **Sign-off**: human, warm close

### Formatting
- Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences)
- White space between sections
- Bullet points for scannability
- Bold sparingly
- Mobile-first

### Length Guidelines
- Transactional: 50-125 words
- Educational: 150-300 words
- Story-driven: 300-500 words

### CTA Rules
- Buttons for primary actions
- Links for secondary actions
- One clear primary CTA per email
- Button text: [Action] + [Outcome]

## Timing & Segmentation

### Timing
- Welcome email: immediately
- Early sequence: 1-2 days apart
- Nurture: 2-4 days apart
- Long-term: weekly or bi-weekly
- B2B: avoid weekends
- Send at local time

### Segmentation
- **By behavior**: openers vs non-openers, clickers, active vs inactive
- **By stage**: trial vs paid, new vs long-term, engaged vs at-risk
- **By profile**: industry/role, use case, company size

## Metrics & Testing

| Metric | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| Open rate | 20-40% |
| Click rate | 2-5% |
| Unsubscribe rate | Under 0.5% |

**What to test** (in priority order): subject lines, send times, email length, CTA placement and copy, personalization level, sequence timing

**How**: A/B test one variable, sufficient sample size, document learnings
