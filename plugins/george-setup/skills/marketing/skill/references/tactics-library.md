## Selection Guide

### By Stage
- **Pre-launch**: waitlist referrals, early access, Product Hunt prep, landing page
- **Early stage**: content, SEO, community, founder-led sales, social
- **Growth**: paid acquisition, partnerships, events, email sequences
- **Scale**: brand, international, acquisitions, conferences

### By Budget
- **Free**: content, SEO, community, social media, open source
- **Low**: targeted ads, sponsorships, free tools, email
- **Medium**: events, partnerships, PR, influencers
- **High**: acquisitions, conferences, brand campaigns, OOH

### By Timeline
- **Quick wins (days)**: ads, email, social posts, giveaways
- **Medium-term (weeks-months)**: content, SEO, community building
- **Long-term (months-years)**: brand, thought leadership, platform effects, compounding
